# Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We as members, contributors, and maintainers pledge to make participation in the
JPROT project a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body
size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender
identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status,
nationality, personal appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity and
orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming,
diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our standards

Examples of behaviour that contributes to a positive environment:

- Being welcoming to newcomers, including those whose first pull request is
  slightly off and who are working from the contributing guide for the first time.
- Being respectful of differing opinions, viewpoints, and experiences. A pull
  request that rewrites your code is still a contribution.
- Giving and gracefully accepting constructive feedback. Accepting that a
  proposed design can be declined, and asking why, is the whole point.
- Focusing on what is best for the project and its users, not on winning an
  argument.
- Taking responsibility for your mistakes, and correcting them without drama.

Examples of unacceptable behaviour:

- Sexualised language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind.
- Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks.
- Public or private harassment.
- Publishing others' private information — a physical or email address, for
  example — without their explicit permission.
- Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a
  professional setting.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when
an individual is officially representing the project in public spaces.

## Reporting

If you see or experience behaviour that violates this Code of Conduct, report it
to the maintainers by opening a
[GitHub issue](https://github.com/Moaaz-i/JPROT/issues) or, if the report
concerns a security vulnerability, following [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Reports are handled confidentially. You will not be required to confront the
other person, and you will not be penalised for making a report in good faith
that turns out not to be upheld. Please include as much detail as you are
comfortable providing, and keep the specifics out of public channels.

Maintainers who do not follow or enforce this Code of Conduct in good faith may
face temporary or permanent repercussions as determined by other maintainers.

## Enforcement

Maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing these standards, and
will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behaviour
that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

They have the right and responsibility to remove, edit, or reject comments,
commits, code, issues, and other contributions that are not aligned with this
Code of Conduct, and will communicate reasons for moderation decisions when
appropriate.
